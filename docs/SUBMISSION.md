# Submission draft

**Tagline:** Shorts you can trust: sourced, fact-checked science Shorts in English, Pidgin, Yoruba, Hausa and Igbo.

**Description (~150 words):**
muse-shorts is an agentic creator pipeline that turns a science topic into a publish-ready vertical Short whose every claim is backed by a source. Meta's open-weights Muse Spark Open (via Baseten) orchestrates the tools: it gathers Wikipedia and PubMed evidence, writes a script where each line cites a verbatim quote, and runs a claim critic that rejects and rewrites unsupported lines. Health topics need 100% of claims verified. Muse Image draws each frame, and SAM 3.1 acts as a verification gate, so a label appears only if SAM finds that structure. The verified script is translated into Nigerian Pidgin, Yoruba, Hausa or Igbo, checked by back-translation, and voiced with WazobiaVoice open-weights TTS. Captions are matched to the voice, outputs are fingerprinted, and nothing is exported until a human creator approves it. It's built for Nigeria, where health misinformation spreads fastest in the languages good sources ignore.
